RemoteCC — Privacy

Updated: November 16, 2026 (visit counting added)

RemoteCC lets you control Claude Code on your own computer from a browser or the AI Code Remote iPhone app. There are no accounts and none of your data in the cloud.

Where your data lives

  • On your computer: the session list, message queue, artifacts, files you send and secrets (encrypted, key held in the system keychain) live in ~/.remotecc. Conversation history belongs to Claude Code (~/.claude).
  • On your phone / browser: only the pairing key for your computer (Keychain / IndexedDB). No copy of conversations or secrets.

The relay

Your phone or browser and your computer talk through the relay at relay.remotecc.dev (Cloudflare). Everything is end-to-end encrypted (X25519 + AES-256-GCM) between your device and your computer; the relay holds no keys, can't read anything and stores no messages. It only sees when data passes, how large it is, and keeps a hash of the pairing token. If the computer is off, the app shows it as offline — messages are not held. When your device is on the same network or Tailscale, it can talk to the computer directly (still end-to-end encrypted, a fresh key per connection) without the relay.

Push notifications

When Claude finishes, your computer asks the relay to send a notification through Apple. The text is a fixed phrase (“Claude is done”, “Claude is waiting for your answer”, “Claude hit an error”) plus a random session id to open the right session — no conversation content.

Claude

Claude Code runs on your computer with your own Anthropic account; RemoteCC sends nothing to any other AI provider.

Controls

  • Revoke a device, or “Reset pairing” (disconnect everything), on the local page http://127.0.0.1:7790 on your computer.
  • “Forget this Mac” in the app / web.
  • Uninstall: bash ~/.remotecc/app/install.sh uninstall; delete ~/.remotecc to remove all data.

Automatic updates

The bridge on your computer checks remotecc.dev about once an hour for a new version. It only installs files signed with RemoteCC's release key, waits until no task is running, and rolls back on its own if the new version fails to start. The check sends no account data, project files or conversations. Turn it off from the menu bar icon (“Install updates automatically”) or with remotecc settings auto-update off.

Visit counting on remotecc.dev

To know which pages help and where visitors come from, remotecc.dev counts visits in aggregate. No cookies, no fingerprinting, no IP addresses, no user id, no third-party analytics. For each page load, a minute the page is open and visible, or a click on a link that leaves the page, the site records: the page path, the referring site's domain, a utm_source tag if the link had one, the country (from Cloudflare) and the kind of client (desktop, phone, crawler). The page adds a random id that lives only in that browser tab, so minutes can be added up per visit; it is never stored on your device and can't be linked to you. The site also counts downloads of the installer and update checks from bridges (the same fields, nothing about your computer). It never sees anything you do with Claude Code — that is end-to-end encrypted. Nothing is counted if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, and the web app served by your own computer (http://127.0.0.1:7791) counts nothing at all.
