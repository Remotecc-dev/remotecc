Code with gestures
and your voice

Answer Claude Code without typing: nod to agree, hold up fingers to choose, or just talk. When it is done, Claude reads you a summary.

Where to turn it on

+/400k
  1. Open a Claude Code session.
  2. Tap the hand button next to the context-compact button (400k) to switch it on or off. Options: app → Settings → Vibecoding; web → the gear button on the vibe bar.
  3. Pick a mode and allow Camera / Microphone when asked.

Two modes

Gestures + voice

The front camera reads your head and hands on the device. Hold up 4 fingers or tap the mic to talk.

Voice only

No camera, easier on the battery. The mic listens continuously — stop talking for ~2 seconds and it sends.

Voice commands

Speak normally

Pause ~2 seconds and it is sent to Claude, as if you typed it.

“Stop” · “Dừng lại” · “Ngắt”

Interrupts the turn Claude is running.

“Im lặng” · “Ngừng đọc”

Stops reading the summary.

“Đọc lại”

Plays the last summary again.

“Tắt vibe”

Exits vibecoding.

Tips

Before sending there is a 6-second countdown (default, adjustable in the gear menu; the app vibrates) — shake your head or tap Cancel to abort.
Prop the device up, face 30–50 cm away, with enough light in front.
When Claude finishes, it reads the first 1–2 sentences and the last question (if any), using a built-in voice on your device.
Camera images are processed on the device and never sent anywhere.
The camera uses more battery — for long sessions use Voice only. The web works best in Safari (Apple speech recognition) or Chrome.
